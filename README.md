# HackClub_DeskBuddy
Desk Buddy is a project to build a physical AI companion for my desk that helps me focus and manage my day without having to constantly manage another app. It combines a small desk device with a touchscreen, physical buttons, LEDs, and a web dashboard. The idea is to have it track focus sessions, detect when I get distracted, and use AI to understand whether what I’m doing is actually related to my current task. It would also connect to my calendar, notes, and tasks to help organize my schedule, remind me of things, and adjust when plans change. Other features include locking my phone during focus sessions, emergency unlocking through NFC, contextual alarms, sleep modes, and quickly capturing things to organize later. I want to build something that feels less like another productivity app and more like a personal assistant that helps me stay on track, avoid burnout, and spend less time managing my productivity and more time actually doing things.
